Worship

Word, table, and song.

Share God’s love in person — or via livestream from a computer or phone. Holy Communion every Sunday. You do not need to be Lutheran to come.

Sunday · 9:00 AM

Sunday school & breakfast

Fellowship Hall. Intergenerational study of the day’s readings, then crafts and activities by age. Breakfast is served. All ages welcome.

Sunday · 10:00 AM

Worship & Holy Communion

Sanctuary. Hymns, liturgy, choir, bells, organ, and piano. Communion every week. Facebook Live at 10:00 AM.

Wednesday · 4:00 PM

Choir practice

New voices welcome. C. Tyler Odom directs. Bell choir rehearses Fridays at 11:00 AM — no audition, reading music not required.

Livestream

Worship from home.

Sundays at 10:00 AM we go live on Facebook. You do not need an account to watch live or recorded services. If the stream fails, call the office or Pastor Ben.
