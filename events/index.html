Events calendar

The weeks ahead.

Sunday school, worship, Back-to-Church Sunday, choir, bells, confirmation, and the monthly mission. Call the office if you need a ride.

  1. Sun, Sep 6, 2026

    Sunday school returns · breakfast served

    9:00 AM · Fellowship Hall

    Intergenerational study of the day’s readings, then crafts by age. Breakfast begins this Sunday. Help in the kitchen — see Andi.

  2. Sun, Sep 6, 2026

    Sunday worship with Holy Communion

    10:00 AM · Sanctuary · 2600 Atlantic Ave

    In person and on Facebook Live. POP Band rehearses after worship.

  3. Mon, Sep 7, 2026

    Labor Day — office closed

    All day · Church office

    The office is closed for the holiday. Men’s property group still meets at 9:00 AM.

  4. Wed, Sep 9, 2026

    Choir practice

    4:00 PM · Church

    Weekly on Wednesdays. New voices welcome. Tyler Odom directs.

  5. Fri, Sep 11, 2026

    Handbell choir

    11:00 AM · Church

    Three-octave choir. No audition. Reading music is not required. All ages.

  6. Sat, Sep 12, 2026

    Confirmation class

    10:00 AM – noon · Church

    Nine confirmands are on track for confirmation in October. Makeup class September 19.

  7. Sun, Sep 13, 2026

    Back-to-Church Sunday · God’s Work. Our Hands.

    10:00 AM · Sanctuary & Fellowship Hall

    ELCA synod Sunday of service plus a potluck luncheon. Bring a dish, a neighbor, and canned goods for the narthex.

  8. Fri, Sep 18, 2026

    Barnabas Center food drive

    11:00 AM – 5:00 PM · Publix, Fernandina Beach

    Collecting for neighbors in crisis. Continues Saturday the 19th at Walmart and Publix.

  9. Sun, Sep 20, 2026

    Youth luncheon after worship

    After 10:00 AM worship · Fellowship Hall

    Lightkeepers youth — service, fellowship, and the road toward the 2027 ELCA Youth Gathering.

  10. Sun, Sep 27, 2026

    Birthday Sunday

    After worship · Fellowship Hall

    Cupcakes and snacks for October birthdays. Lightkeepers host. All are invited.

  11. Tue, Oct 6, 2026

    Adult Bible study · Ecclesiastes

    3:30 PM · Holy Grounds Coffee House, Amelia Island Parkway

    Tuesdays in October (6, 13, 20, 27). Wisdom literature — “Turn! Turn! Turn!” behind the Speedway at 8th and Amelia Island Parkway.

  12. Tue, Oct 13, 2026

    Prayer shawl group

    1:00 PM · Narthex

    Second Tuesday each month. Crochet, knit, quilt, sew — gifts for baptisms, Habitat homes, and members who need cheering. Robin Howell.

  13. Fri, Oct 2, 2026

    Potter’s Daughters ladies group

    Monthly · Church

    Study, friendship, and hands-on mission. Activities return in October. All women welcome.

Recurring in the building: Men’s property group Mondays 9:00 AM. AA Mondays 12:00–1:30 PM and Saturdays 9:00–11:30 AM.
